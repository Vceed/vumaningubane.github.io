My Journey

My Story

I was born in Johannesburg in 2006. At a young age, I relocated to the rural areas of eMsinga in KwaZulu-Natal, where I began my early educational journey.


Growing up, I had a strong interest in becoming an actor. Creativity and storytelling were already part of the person I wanted to become.


My educational journey started at Makhandana Primary School. I later attended Nomafu High School before completing my matric at KwaNdunge High School in 2023.


When I entered the University of Johannesburg, my interests began to expand. I developed a strong interest in transportation, logistics and supply chain, while continuing to hold onto my passion for creativity.


My university journey has allowed me to explore entrepreneurship and innovation through collaborative projects such as TwinFleet. It has also given me opportunities to develop leadership and communication skills.


In 2026, I became a UJ mentor supporting first-year students, an experience that continues to develop my leadership, communication and ability to encourage others.


At the same time, I continue to explore my creative side. I have attended a Hlophe Casting Agency workshop and have gained experience as a background actor in Homecoming.


My journey is still being written. I am determined to keep learning, creating, building and exploring opportunities across transportation, entrepreneurship and the creative world.
